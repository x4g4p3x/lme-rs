# Ten-defect correctness batch, 2026-10-10

The baseline was `c92c90998938a30cdd3e72bb1b34600b446f0874` on Windows
with static MKL. Nine of the final primary regressions in
[test_bug_hunt_batch.rs](../../tests/test_bug_hunt_batch.rs) compiled and failed
against unchanged production code. The tenth, natural-spline extrapolation, was
reproduced separately before changing the spline implementation: prediction at
`x=-4` was `5`, while the fitted affine function requires `-10`.
Related symptoms and additional boundary controls count toward the same
underlying defect.

An initial CV candidate was withdrawn when broader validation established that
independent split columns are intentionally supported. Its proposed restriction
was removed, the misleading documentation was corrected, and compatibility
controls were retained. It is not included in the ten-defect count.

| # | Confirmed defect and independent expectation | Cause and fix | Primary regression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sparse weights `(x, 1), (x, 2)` produced `2*x`; their linear combination is `3*x`. | Sparse contrast construction overwrote earlier entries. Sum repeated weights in indexed and named contrasts. | `sparse_contrast_repeated_coefficients_add` |
| 2 | A model fitted with `y ~ .` tried to use a new, all-null metadata column during prediction. Prediction must reuse the fitted predictors and order. | Dot expansion was recomputed from the new frame. Store ordered source names in the training basis; also use them for marginal-mean source selection. | `dot_prediction_uses_training_sources_and_order` |
| 3 | Finite predictors whose product with a fitted slope exceeded `f64` returned successful infinite predictions. Unrepresentable outputs require an error. | Only design inputs were checked. Validate population, conditional, nonlinear, and inverse-link output vectors. | `prediction_rejects_unrepresentable_linear_mean` |
| 4 | Simulation accepted a negative Poisson mean by replacing it with epsilon. Invalid distribution means require an error; valid zero intensity and probability endpoints must retain their distributions. | Mean clipping hid domain violations and perturbed boundaries. Validate means, remove clipping, handle zero Poisson intensity exactly, and reject non-finite draws. | `simulation_rejects_invalid_distribution_means` |
| 5 | Binomial preparation accepted a trial count of `2^64`, although it cannot be represented by the sampler's `u64` trial parameter. | Float-to-integer conversion saturated silently. Check the integer domain before conversion, in preparation and simulation. Fractional precision weights retain their existing interpretation. | `binomial_trials_reject_saturating_integer_casts` |
| 6 | Percentile summaries accepted a converged NaN coefficient draw and returned invalid uncertainty. Percentiles require finite input estimates and draws. | Bootstrap summaries lacked finite-value validation; invalid residual variances also fell through to the no-dispersion representation. Reject invalid summaries and preserve legitimate zero residual variance. | `bootstrap_summary_rejects_nonfinite_draws` |
| 7 | A natural-spline model fitted to `y=2+3*x` predicted `5` at `x=-4` instead of `-10`. Natural splines contain affine functions and extend linearly beyond their boundary knots. | B-spline evaluation returned zero outside the training knot span. Extend each boundary row by its tangent before applying the stored natural-spline projection. | `natural_spline_prediction_extends_linearly_beyond_training_boundaries` |
| 8 | Robust inference accepted reversed predictor rows, combining the new design with old residuals and covariance. | Only row count was checked. Require the original fixed design and ordering, allowing column-scaled floating-point noise when reapplying stored bases. | `robust_inference_rejects_mismatched_training_rows` |
| 9 | The log inverse at eta `100` returned `exp(30)`; at eta `-100` it returned `exp(-30)`. The inverse and derivative are both `exp(eta)` throughout the representable range. | Arbitrary predictor clipping and epsilon floors truncated the link. Use native logarithm and exponential operations; prediction output validation reports overflow. | `log_inverse_link_preserves_representable_range` |
| 10 | Complementary log-log transformation of probability `1e-12` lost accuracy through subtractive cancellation. | Direct `1-p` and `1-exp(...)` calculations lost significant digits. Use `ln_1p` and `exp_m1`, with a safe limiting derivative in the upper tail. | `cloglog_link_is_accurate_for_small_probabilities` |

Controls cover contrast cancellation and unknown names; absent dot predictors;
known categorical levels in a one-row prediction; irrelevant marginal-mean
metadata; sequential and parallel simulation; invalid means across families;
deterministic binomial endpoints with large trial counts; zero Poisson intensity;
representable and fractional trial weights; non-binomial precision weights;
known percentile limits; ignored nonconverged draws; zero residual variance;
separate and transformed/nested CV split sources; natural-spline extrapolation
with and without an explicit basis intercept; original polynomial, spline, and dot designs for robust
inference; and actual Poisson response prediction at large finite predictors.
